# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is
best for the people who use the project. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted.

**Reporting.** Email huynhthanh@vtechstudio.tech. Reports are handled confidentially by the maintainer,
who may remove comments, commits or contributions that break these rules and may ban repeat offenders.
